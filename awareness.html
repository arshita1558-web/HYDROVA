Water & health awareness

Simple, general reminders about reducing water-related health risks. Follow local public-health guidance for your area.

01
💧

Use safe drinking water

Use water from a source considered safe by local authorities. If safety is uncertain, follow official local advice on treatment or alternative sources.

02
✋

Wash hands

Wash hands with soap and safe water, especially before preparing food or eating and after using the toilet.

03
🥗

Handle food safely

Keep food and utensils clean, prepare food with safe water, and store food in a way that limits contamination.

04
🩺

Seek medical advice

Seek qualified medical advice for persistent symptoms or concerns. Signs of severe dehydration or other serious illness need urgent care.
